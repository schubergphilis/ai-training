<Slide part="Why">

## Why the project exists

Generative AI reached office work in a short time, and most people got no
training in how these tools work or fail.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="A common starting point">
    A group that learns the same words and the same basic model of the
    tools can discuss a result, a risk or a working method with each other.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Safe use and real use together">
    People stop checking a machine that does most of a task. So safety
    comes first, and every area returns to checking the output.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open content">
    Anyone may run the courses, change a lesson to fit their own tools, or
    study alone with only a browser.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## The parts of a lesson

Each lesson has the same parts in the same order. It is one page of 10
to 25 minutes, plus an exercise that the learner does in their own tools.

</Slide>

<Slide part="How a lesson teaches">

## Checkpoints test one objective each

A wrong answer gets feedback, and a hint asks a question about the
mistake. The page never reveals the answer.

<FromAbout id="figure-checkpoint-kinds" />

</Slide>

<Slide part="How a lesson teaches">

## What the learner finished comes back

- **Retrieval practice.** Recall an idea again, instead of reading it again.
- **Spaced review.** The gaps between reviews grow.
- **Habits.** One change in real work, asked about again a few days later.
